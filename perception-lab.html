S010 / AGENCY / GLOBULE PERCEPTION

Power does not require war.

A globule estimates a neighbor's relative power and decides whether engaging is worth the energy and risk. Staying homeostatic is a valid action. No one is obligated to eat 99 equal-ranked peers just because a larger aggregate is possible.

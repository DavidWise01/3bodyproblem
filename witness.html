Originality boundary

The seven story stations are original commentary and compact symbolic interpretations of the work's broad events and themes. They are not the novel's text, do not include reconstructed chapters, and cannot replace reading the source. Original speech is creative rather than quotation.

Each independent witness would require a distinct source ancestry and a grounded observation; three replicated web copies of one text do not count as three witnesses.

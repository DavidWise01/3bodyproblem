S008 · GENERATIONAL POWER / NINE PEERS / ONE SEED

Eat nine.
Become the next power.

One existing globule plus nine equal-rank peers forms a ten-unit aggregate and promotes to the next generational power class. In 2D, the promoted G1 class has a 10×10 addressing envelope; that does not create 90 extra physical units.
